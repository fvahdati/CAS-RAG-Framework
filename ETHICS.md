Ethics and Data Use Statement
Research Context
This repository contains research artefacts developed as part of a PhD study at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS).

The research investigates emotion-reaction analysis of comments on publicly accessible social-experiment videos using Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and the proposed CAS-RAG framework.

Data Sources
The comment datasets used in this repository were collected from publicly accessible online videos for academic research purposes.

The repository does not intentionally publish private, confidential, or personally identifying information.

Responsible Use
The datasets, prompts, and code are provided solely for research, educational, and reproducibility purposes.

Users of this repository are responsible for ensuring that their use of the data complies with:

applicable laws and regulations
platform terms of service
institutional ethics requirements
research integrity principles
Content Notice
Some comments and video topics relate to sensitive social issues, including:

bullying
self-harm
suicide prevention
teen pregnancy
substance misuse
child safety
These materials are included only for research and evaluation purposes.
