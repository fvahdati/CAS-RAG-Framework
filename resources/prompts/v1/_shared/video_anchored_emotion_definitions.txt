Label Definitions (video-anchored — Stage 2):
Classify the dominant reaction a viewer expresses after watching this social experiment.

Shock: Surprise or emotional disturbance at what happened in the video.
Disbelief: Difficulty accepting that the behaviour, participants, or setup is real or believable.
Anger: Moral outrage at unfair treatment, abuse, bullying, manipulation, or humiliation shown in the video — including personal stories where abusive behaviour is the dominant focus.
Frustration: Annoyance, helplessness, or emotional dissatisfaction as the dominant reaction toward the situation.
Empathy: Compassion, support, admiration, or kindness expressed toward vulnerable people or those who helped — NOT merely sharing a similar personal experience.
None: Neutral factual comments, jokes without clear emotion, generic reactions without a reliable link to the video, or reactions outside the five emotion categories.

Empathy vs Frustration (important — Stage 2):
• Choose Empathy when the comment praises people who helped, admires kindness, or expresses compassion toward someone in distress — even if mistreatment is mentioned hypothetically.
• Choose Frustration when annoyance, helplessness, or dissatisfaction with the situation is the dominant reaction.
